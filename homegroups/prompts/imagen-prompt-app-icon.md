# Google Imagen Prompt for Homegroups App Icon

## Primary Prompt

**Homegroups is a mobile application built for 12-step recovery homegroups (like Alcoholics Anonymous and Narcotics Anonymous). The app provides essential tools for group communication, meeting management, and basic operations like treasury management, all while strictly adhering to the principles of anonymity and group autonomy that are central to these fellowships. The app solves common operational challenges faced by recovery groups without resorting to public social media platforms, offering a private, secure, and focused environment. It emphasizes privacy-first design, community support, trust, and connection. Create an app icon that visually represents this concept - a recovery community connected through technology while maintaining anonymity and support. The icon must be simple, work at small sizes (as small as 20x20 pixels), and be suitable for both iOS and Android platforms. Use the app's color palette: primary blue (#1976D2), light blue (#90CAF9), white backgrounds, and optional green (#4CAF50) for positive elements.**

## Key Requirements for App Icons

- **Simplicity**: Must be recognizable and clear at very small sizes (down to 20x20 pixels)
- **Square Format**: Standard app icon dimensions (1024x1024 for App Store, various sizes for Android)
- **No Text**: Icons typically don't include text (app name appears separately)
- **High Contrast**: Should work on both light and dark backgrounds
- **Scalable**: Design should work at multiple sizes without losing meaning
- **Platform Guidelines**: Should follow iOS and Android design guidelines

## App Values to Convey

- **Community & Connection**: People coming together in support
- **Anonymity & Privacy**: Respect for individual privacy and group traditions
- **Trust & Support**: Safe, secure environment for recovery communities
- **Technology as a Tool**: Digital connection that serves the community
- **Warmth & Welcome**: Supportive, non-clinical, accessible

## Color Palette Reference

- **Primary Blue**: #1976D2 (main brand color)
- **Light Blue**: #90CAF9 (secondary/accent)
- **Dark Blue**: #1565C0 (optional for depth/shadow)
- **Green**: #4CAF50 (optional accent for positive elements)
- **Background**: #FFFFFF (white) or transparent
- **Neutral Grays**: Available for subtle elements (#757575, #9E9E9E)

## Design Considerations

- Avoid complex details that won't be visible at small sizes
- Use bold, simple shapes and symbols
- Consider how the icon looks on both light and dark backgrounds
- Ensure the design is unique and doesn't resemble other common app icons
- The icon should be immediately recognizable as related to community/connection
- Maintain privacy focus - avoid showing identifiable faces or personal details

## Usage Notes

This icon will be used for:

- iOS App Store (1024x1024 pixels required)
- Android Play Store (512x512 pixels minimum, various sizes needed)
- App launcher on user devices (various sizes from 20x20 to 512x512)
- App settings and system UI elements

The icon should be professional, trustworthy, and immediately communicate the app's purpose of connecting recovery communities while maintaining privacy and anonymity.
