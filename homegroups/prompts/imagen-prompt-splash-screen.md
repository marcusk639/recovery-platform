## Primary Prompt

**Homegroups is a mobile application built for 12-step recovery homegroups (like Alcoholics Anonymous and Narcotics Anonymous). The app provides essential tools for group communication, meeting management, and basic operations like treasury management, all while strictly adhering to the principles of anonymity and group autonomy that are central to these fellowships. The app solves common operational challenges faced by recovery groups without resorting to public social media platforms, offering a private, secure, and focused environment. It emphasizes privacy-first design, community support, trust, and connection. Create a splash screen design that will be the first thing users see when opening the app. The splash screen should be simple, elegant, and immediately communicate the app's purpose of connecting recovery communities while maintaining privacy and anonymity. Use the app's color palette: primary blue (#1976D2), light blue (#90CAF9), white and light gray backgrounds (#FFFFFF, #F5F5F5), and optional green accents (#4CAF50).**

## Key Requirements for Splash Screens

- **Simplicity**: Clean, uncluttered design that loads quickly
- **Brand Identity**: Should immediately communicate the app's purpose and values
- **Vertical Format**: Typically portrait orientation for mobile apps
- **Centered Design**: Logo and key elements should be centered
- **Minimal Text**: Usually just the app name or logo, sometimes a tagline
- **Fast Loading**: Should be simple enough to display quickly
- **Professional**: First impression matters - should be polished and trustworthy

## App Values to Convey

- **Community & Connection**: People coming together in support
- **Anonymity & Privacy**: Respect for individual privacy and group traditions
- **Trust & Support**: Safe, secure environment for recovery communities
- **Technology as a Tool**: Digital connection that serves the community
- **Warmth & Welcome**: Supportive, non-clinical, accessible
- **Calm & Reassuring**: Should feel peaceful and supportive, not overwhelming

## Color Palette Reference

- **Primary Blue**: #1976D2 (main brand color)
- **Light Blue**: #90CAF9 (secondary/accent)
- **Dark Blue**: #1565C0 (optional for depth/shadow)
- **Green**: #4CAF50 (optional accent for positive elements)
- **Background**: #FFFFFF (white) or #F5F5F5 (light gray)
- **Neutral Grays**: Available for text and subtle elements (#757575, #9E9E9E)

## Design Considerations

- Keep it simple - splash screens are typically displayed for 1-3 seconds
- Center the app logo or name prominently
- Use plenty of white space for a clean, uncluttered feel
- Consider subtle background patterns or gradients for visual interest
- Avoid complex illustrations that might slow loading
- Ensure text (if any) is readable and minimal
- The design should work in both light and dark mode if applicable
- Should feel welcoming and supportive, not clinical or institutional

## Usage Notes

This splash screen will be used as:

- The initial loading screen when the app launches
- The first visual impression users have of the app
- A brief screen shown while the app initializes (typically 1-3 seconds)
- Part of the app's onboarding experience

The splash screen should be professional, trustworthy, and immediately communicate the app's purpose of connecting recovery communities while maintaining privacy and anonymity. It should feel warm, welcoming, and supportive - setting the right tone for users entering the app.
