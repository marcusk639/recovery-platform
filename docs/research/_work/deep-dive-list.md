## Scoring

`priority = risk × leverage × checkability` (each factor 1–5 from the ledger).

- **risk** — how badly a wrong assertion damages the business case.
- **leverage** — how much the whole strategy/sequence hinges on it.
- **checkability** — how settle-able it is with external evidence (high = worth spending budget on).

Priority ranges 16–125. We sort descending and take the top tier (priority ≥ 64,
ranks 1–21), then **explicitly add** bias-required claims the plan names that fall
below the raw cut (Phase 2 step 3): **D-11 IAP** and the **12-step treasurer / 7th-Tradition
WTP GAP**. Final set: **24 claims** (within the 15–30 band).

**Bias targets confirmed present** (per plan): quantitative projections, regulatory gates
(D-10 anti-kickback, D-11 IAP, D-12/HIPAA, Medicaid PSS), the network-effect thesis, and the
three named research GAPS (Medicaid PSS billing, detox IAP-vs-web billing, 12-step treasurer WTP).
See the coverage note under the table.
