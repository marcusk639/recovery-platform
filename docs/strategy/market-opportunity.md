## Recovery Ecosystem — Product & Market Intelligence Brief

> **Purpose:** Claude Code development context document. Synthesizes market research, competitive intelligence, product strategy, GTM findings, and feature requirements for a three-product recovery technology ecosystem.

### 1. Overview: The Three-Product Ecosystem

This project comprises three interconnected applications targeting the addiction recovery continuum. Each app is independently viable but significantly more defensible and valuable as a combined platform.

| App                             | Core Purpose                                                                                                                | Primary Customer                                                  | Current Status                                       |
| ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| **12-Step Community App**       | Group management, meeting finder (100K+ pre-seeded meetings), service positions, treasury tools                             | AA/NA/12-step groups, intergroups, individual members in recovery | Built, MVP stage                                     |
| **Sober Living App**            | Resident management, phases, accountability, payment processing, directory (sober living homes), Oxford House model support | Sober living operators (traditional + Oxford House model)         | Built, MVP stage, traditional homes showing interest |
| **Aftercare Management System** | Post-discharge outcome tracking, discharge planning, sober living referrals, alumni engagement — sold to treatment centers  | Addiction treatment center clinical and administrative staff      | To be built                                          |

#### The Ecosystem Logic

The three apps map directly to the **ASAM Continuum of Care** — the clinical standard for addiction treatment level-of-care transitions:

```
Detox → Residential → PHP → IOP → Outpatient → [SOBER LIVING APP] → [12-STEP APP]
                                                        ↑
                                              [AFTERCARE SYSTEM bridges the gap]
```

**No competitor in the market connects these three phases digitally.** Current treatment center software (Kipu Health, Sunwave, LightningStep, Opus EHR) treats the patient journey as ending at discharge. Sober living software and 12-step apps are completely separate industries with no integration. This gap is the strategic opportunity.

## Next Step Recovery — Market Opportunity Analysis

**Prepared:** May 2026
**Business:** Next Step Recovery (nextsteprecovery.com)
**Stage:** Pre-revenue / just launched
**Geography:** United States (Year 1–3)

### Executive Summary

The US substance use disorder (SUD) ecosystem has a structural 80% treatment gap: 52.6 million people needed SUD treatment in 2024, but only 10.2 million received it (SAMHSA 2024 NSDUH). Next Step Recovery occupies a specific, underserved niche at the intersection of three growing markets:

1. **Peer support for acute withdrawal navigation** (~$40M TAM)
2. **Family member education and support** (~$12M TAM)
3. **B2B patient experience consulting for treatment facilities** (~$25M TAM)

**Combined TAM: ~$120M | SAM: ~$27M | SOM (Year 3): ~$130K–$540K**

### Total Addressable Market

| Segment                                   | TAM         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| B2C — Withdrawal support calls + packages | $11.7M      |
| B2C — Family member products and calls    | $10.8M      |
| Digital products / information products   | $5.0M       |
| B2B — Treatment center consulting         | $9.0M       |
| **Total TAM (Bottom-Up)**                 | **~$36.5M** |

Working TAM: **$40M** (bottom-up $36.5M, top-down implied $61M — conservative figure used)

Working SAM: **~$10M**

### Year 3 SOM

- Base Case (Solo Founder): 1.3% of SAM = **$130,000**
- Small Team (2–3 Practitioners): 3–4% of SAM = **$300,000–$400,000**
- Platform/Marketplace Scenario: 8–12% of SAM = **$800K–$1.2M**

### Key Differentiators

1. **Withdrawal-specific focus** — Addresses acute crisis moment, not long-term sobriety maintenance
2. **Navigation role** — "Help me figure out what to do next" vs. ongoing sobriety support
3. **Clear non-clinical scope** — 16 defined red-flag conditions trigger immediate escalation
4. **B2B patient experience consulting** — No direct competitor in this adjacent market
5. **Founder's specific expertise** — Deep knowledge of withdrawal physiology and patient/family needs
