Why Regroup?

Regroup Sober Living was built by people in sober living for people in sober living. We designed it to solve problems faced by not only the managers of sober living homes, but by the residents they are trying to help.

Fully customizable

Customize the way your home operates. Define the requirements for your residents, then let the app do the rest.

Meetings

Using our meeting finder, your residents can find recovery meetings in your area, get directions using Google Maps, and check into them via GPS.

Facilities

Manage the living space in your home, including maintenance issues, chores, rooms and bed assignments.
