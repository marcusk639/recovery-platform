## 1. Current State Crisis Analysis

### Current Pricing Structure

```
Base: $10/house/month
Per-Resident: $1/resident/month

Examples:
├─ 6 residents  = $16/month = $192/year
├─ 10 residents = $20/month = $240/year
├─ 15 residents = $25/month = $300/year
└─ 20 residents = $30/month = $360/year
```

### Problem 1: Unsustainable Unit Economics ❌

**Annual Cost Structure (Minimum Viable Business):**

```
Required Annual Costs:
├─ Developer salary (1 FTE): $120,000
├─ Support (part-time): $30,000
├─ Infrastructure (Firebase, Stripe): $6,000
├─ Marketing: $24,000
├─ Tools & SaaS: $6,000
├─ Legal & Compliance: $4,000
└─ Total: $190,000/year

Current Revenue (assuming 50 active houses at $20/mo avg):
50 houses × $20/month = $1,000/month = $12,000/year

Annual Deficit: -$178,000/year 🔴
```

**The business loses $178K/year at current pricing.**

**To break even at current pricing, you would need:**
```
$190,000 / $20 per house / 12 months = 792 houses
```

**792 houses is 3.2% of total market (25,000 houses)** - unrealistic without massive marketing budget (which you can't afford at $20/month).

---

### Problem 2: Signals Low Quality ⚠️

**Customer Psychology of Pricing:**

| Price Point | Customer Perception | Example Products |
|-------------|---------------------|------------------|
| $5-20/month | "Consumer app or hobby project" | Netflix, Spotify |
| $50-100/month | "Small business SaaS" | Mailchimp, Quickbooks |
| $100-500/month | "Professional B2B software" | Salesforce, HubSpot |
| $500+/month | "Enterprise solution" | SAP, Oracle |

**At $20/month, customers think:**
- "Will this company be around next year?"
- "Is support even available?"
- "This must be basic or missing features"
- "They'll probably shut down or get acquired"

**Higher pricing = higher perceived value and commitment to the product.**

---

### Problem 3: Captures Only 1-2% of Value Delivered ⚠️

**Value Provided to Average Sober Living House:**

```
Monthly Value Delivered:
├─ Admin time saved: 15 hours × $50/hr = $750
├─ GPS meeting fraud prevention = $200
├─ Reduced disputes (clearer records) = $150
├─ Compliance tracking (avoid violations) = $300
└─ Total Value Delivered: $1,400/month

Current Price: $20/month
Value Capture Rate: 20/1400 = 1.4%

Customer ROI: (1400-20)/20 = 6,900%
```

**Customers get 69X ROI** - you're leaving massive value on the table.

**Industry standard value capture: 10-20% of value delivered**

At 10% capture: $140/month
At 15% capture: $210/month
At 20% capture: $280/month

**Even at $129/month, customers still get 10X ROI** - this is sustainable pricing.

---

### Problem 4: Cannot Afford Customer Success ❌

**Support Economics:**

```
Scenario: 100 customers at current pricing

Monthly Support Hours:
├─ Average support time: 2 hours per customer per month
├─ Total support hours: 200 hours/month
└─ Support cost at $30/hr: $6,000/month

Monthly Revenue:
100 customers × $20/month = $2,000/month

Monthly Net: $2,000 - $6,000 = -$4,000/month

You LOSE $4,000/month just on support costs.
```

**At recommended pricing ($100/month average):**

```
Monthly Revenue: 100 customers × $100 = $10,000/month
Support Cost: $6,000/month
Monthly Net: +$4,000/month

You can afford a full-time support person and still profit.
```

---

### Problem 5: No Growth Budget ❌

**Marketing Economics:**

To acquire 100 houses per year with digital marketing:

```
Industry CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost): $200-500 per B2B customer

100 houses × $300 CAC = $30,000/year marketing budget

At current pricing:
Monthly revenue per house: $20
Annual revenue per house: $240
LTV (3-year retention): $240 × 3 = $720
LTV/CAC ratio: $720 / $300 = 2.4X

Payback period: 15 months (break-even after 15 months)
```

**This barely works, and requires all customers to stay 3+ years.**

**At recommended pricing ($100/month average):**

```
Annual revenue per house: $1,200
LTV (3-year retention): $1,200 × 3 = $3,600
LTV/CAC ratio: $3,600 / $300 = 12X

Payback period: 3 months

You can spend $30K/year on marketing and still have 12X return.
```

## 2. Value-Based Pricing Framework

### Pricing Philosophy

**Regroup pricing should be based on value delivered, NOT cost to provide.**

**Infrastructure costs are near-zero:**
- Firebase free tier covers first 50 houses
- Blaze plan: ~$300/month at 250 houses
- **Infrastructure is NOT a constraint**

**Value delivered is MASSIVE:**
- Saves 15+ hours/month of admin time
- Prevents meeting fraud (GPS verification)
- Reduces resident conflicts
- Ensures compliance
- Enables better outcomes

**Therefore: Price based on value, not cost.**

---

### Value Calculation by House Type

**Traditional Sober Living House (10 residents, $800/month rent):**

```
Monthly Revenue: 10 residents × $800 = $8,000

Monthly Value from Regroup:
├─ Admin time saved: 15 hrs × $50/hr = $750
├─ Meeting fraud prevention = $200
├─ Better compliance (avoid fines) = $300
├─ Reduced disputes = $150
├─ Better outcomes (lower turnover) = $400
└─ Total Monthly Value: $1,800

As % of revenue: $1,800 / $8,000 = 22.5%
```

**At $129/month Professional tier:**
- Value delivered: $1,800/month
- Price: $129/month
- Value capture: 7.2%
- Customer ROI: (1800-129)/129 = 1,296% (13X)
- As % of revenue: 1.6%

**Customer still gets 13X ROI and pays less than 2% of monthly revenue.**

---

**Oxford House (12 residents, $500/month EES):**

```
Monthly EES Collections: 12 residents × $500 = $6,000

Monthly Value from Regroup:
├─ Treasurer time saved: 5 hrs × $30/hr = $150
├─ Better EES collection (+5%) = $300
├─ Secretary time saved: 3 hrs × $30/hr = $90
├─ Reduced conflicts (transparency) = $100
├─ Charter compliance = $200
└─ Total Monthly Value: $840

As % of collections: $840 / $6,000 = 14%
```

**At $49/month Standard tier:**
- Value delivered: $840/month
- Price: $49/month
- Value capture: 5.8%
- Customer ROI: (840-49)/49 = 1,614% (16X)
- As % of collections: 0.8%

**Oxford House still gets 16X ROI and pays less than 1% of monthly collections.**

---

### Pricing Guardrails

**Maximum Price:** 10% of value delivered
- Ensures customers always get 10X ROI
- Well above industry standard (3-5X ROI)

**Minimum Price:** $49/month
- Below this, business is unsustainable
- Cannot cover support costs
- Signals low quality

**Sweet Spot:** 5-10% of value delivered
- Customers get 10-20X ROI
- Business is sustainable
- Allows for growth investment

**Recommended range: $49-249/month depending on house size and features**
