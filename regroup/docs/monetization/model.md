# Regroup Pricing Strategy Options

## Comprehensive Pricing Analysis & Recommendations

**Date:** November 29, 2025  
**Current Pricing:** $10/house + $1/resident (~$20/month average)  
**Infrastructure Costs:** ~$0-2/month (within Firebase free tier)

---

## Executive Summary

This document outlines 6 potential pricing strategies for Regroup, from conservative to aggressive. Given near-zero infrastructure costs, **pricing should be based on value delivered** (saves $500-1,000/month in admin time) rather than cost to provide.

### Key Recommendations:

1. **Immediate:** Raise prices to $49-129 range (3-7X increase)
2. **Oxford Houses:** Price at $39-89 (lower than traditional but still sustainable)
3. **Model:** Tiered value-based pricing (not per-resident)
4. **Timeline:** Implement new pricing for new customers immediately, migrate existing customers in 6 months

### Revenue Impact at 250 Houses:

| Pricing Model    | Annual Revenue | Growth Potential    |
| ---------------- | -------------- | ------------------- |
| Current ($10+$1) | $60K           | ❌ Unsustainable    |
| Conservative     | $132K          | ✅ Profitable       |
| **Recommended**  | **$204K**      | **✅ Can scale**    |
| Aggressive       | $312K          | ✅ High margins     |
| Premium          | $456K          | ⚠️ May limit volume |

## Current State Analysis

### What We're Charging Now

```
Base: $10/house
Per Resident: $1/resident

Examples:
├─ 6 residents  = $16/month = $192/year
├─ 10 residents = $20/month = $240/year
```

### Problems with Current Pricing

#### 1. **Doesn't Reflect Value Delivered**

**Value Provided to Customers:**

- Admin time saved: 10-20 hrs/month × $50/hr = **$500-1,000/month**
- GPS meeting verification: Fraud prevention = **$100-300/month**
- Dispute management: Conflict resolution = **$100-200/month**
- Compliance tracking: Avoid violations = **$200-500/month**
- **Total Value: $900-2,000/month**

**Current Capture Rate: 1-2%** (charging $20 for $1,000 value)

#### 2. **Signals Low Quality**

**Customer Psychology:**

- $20/month = Consumer app pricing
- $50-100/month = Small business SaaS
- $100-500/month = Professional B2B software
- $500+ = Enterprise solution

**At $20/month, you signal:** "This is a hobby project that might disappear"

#### 3. **Can't Afford to Scale**

**Support Economics:**

```
100 customers × 2 hours support/month = 200 hours/month

At $20/month:
├─ Revenue: $2,000
├─ Support cost (@$25/hr): $5,000
└─ Net: -$3,000 (LOSING money on support)

At $79/month:
├─ Revenue: $7,900
├─ Support cost: $5,000
└─ Net: +$2,900 (Can afford support!)
```

#### 4. **No Growth Budget**

**Required Annual Costs:**

- Developer salary: $100K
- Support (part-time): $30K
- Marketing: $24K
- Sales/outreach: $12K
- Infrastructure: $3K
- **Total: $169K/year**

**Customers needed:**

- At $20/month: **704 customers**
- At $79/month: **178 customers**

**Which is easier to acquire?** 178 customers at higher price with marketing budget vs. 704 at low price with no budget.

## Risk Analysis & Mitigation

### Risk 1: Existing Customers Churn

**Likelihood:** MEDIUM (15-25% may churn)  
**Impact:** MEDIUM ($300-500 MRR loss if 10 customers × $20/mo)

**Mitigation:**

- ✅ **6-month grandfather period** (goodwill + time to prove value)
- ✅ **Founder pricing discount** (20-30% off forever)
- ✅ **Personal outreach** to largest/most valuable customers
- ✅ **New features** launched during grace period (show value)
- ✅ **Case studies** demonstrating ROI
- ✅ **Money-back guarantee** if not satisfied with new pricing

**Net Impact:** Even with 20% churn, new pricing generates 3-5X more revenue

---

### Risk 2: New Customer Conversion Rate Drops

**Likelihood:** MEDIUM (may drop from 30% to 15-20%)  
**Impact:** MEDIUM (slower growth initially)

**Mitigation:**

- ✅ **Strong value proposition** in marketing
- ✅ **ROI calculator** on website
- ✅ **Free trial** (14-30 days)
- ✅ **Demo videos** showing features
- ✅ **Case studies** with testimonials
- ✅ **Comparison to manual process** (show hours saved)
- ✅ **Money-back guarantee** (30 days)

**Test Strategy:**

- Run A/B tests: $49 vs $69 vs $79
- Track conversion rate by price point
- Adjust if conversion drops below 15%

---

### Risk 3: Competitors Undercut on Price

**Likelihood:** LOW (no direct competitors yet)  
**Impact:** HIGH (if it happens)

**Mitigation:**

- ✅ **Feature differentiation** (GPS verification, 300K meetings, Oxford House support)
- ✅ **Network effects** (inter-house connections)
- ✅ **First-mover advantage** (establish brand)
- ✅ **Customer lock-in** (data, training, workflows)
- ✅ **Annual contracts** (reduces churn)
- ✅ **Oxford House World Services partnership** (official endorsement)

**Response Plan:** If competitor emerges at lower price, compete on value not price. Add features, improve support, build moat.

---

### Risk 4: Oxford Houses Vote "No" on Expense

**Likelihood:** MEDIUM (democratic decision required)  
**Impact:** HIGH (could kill Oxford House adoption)

**Mitigation:**

- ✅ **Lower pricing** ($39-49 vs $69-129 for traditional)
