# Next Step Recovery — Financial Model (2026–2028)

**Prepared:** May 2026  
**Operator:** Solo founder, part-time in Year 1  
**Model horizon:** 3 years (monthly detail Y1, quarterly detail Y2–Y3)  
**Business type:** Service practice + digital products + B2B consulting

---

## Business Model Summary

| Revenue Stream            | Product                             | Price                    | Status                      |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ------------------------ | --------------------------- |
| **Tier 1 — Fit Check**    | 10-min call                         | Free                     | Live (lead gen)             |
| **Tier 2 — Support Call** | 30-min call                         | $50                      | Live                        |
| **Tier 3 — Family Call**  | 60-min call                         | $125–$175                | Launching Q3 2026           |
| **Tier 4 — Nav Package**  | 2-week program                      | $300–$600                | Launching Q2 2027           |
| **Digital Products**      | Guides & worksheets                 | $9.99–$19.99             | Live                        |
| **B2B Consulting**        | Training, journey mapping, advisory | $1,500–$7,500/engagement | Soft-launching (warm leads) |

**Capacity constraint:** 5–8 paid calls/week = 22–32 calls/month maximum across all call tiers.

## Three-Scenario Summary

| Scenario               | Year 1 Revenue | Year 2 Revenue | Year 3 Revenue | Y3 Net Contribution |
| ---------------------- | -------------- | -------------- | -------------- | ------------------- |
| **Conservative (P10)** | $7,200         | $24,000        | $52,000        | ~$29,000            |
| **Base (P50)**         | $13,454        | $42,963        | $89,955        | ~$59,400            |
| **Optimistic (P90)**   | $24,000        | $75,000        | $148,000       | ~$100,000           |

### Conservative Scenario Assumptions

- B2B takes 9–12 months for first engagement (cold pipeline)
- Traffic grows 10% month-over-month
- Tier 3 launches Month 10, Tier 4 never in 3-year window
- Digital product conversion stays at 1%

### Optimistic Scenario Assumptions

- B2B warm leads convert in Month 2–3 ($4,000 first engagement)
- Traffic grows 25%/month via referrals or press mention
- Tier 3 launches Month 5
- Workshop product line launches ($75–$100/seat, 15–20 attendees/session)
- Second B2B retainer added in Year 2

## Revenue Capacity Ceiling Analysis

With a cap of 5–8 calls/week, maximum monthly call revenue by tier mix:

| Scenario                 | Mix                            | Max Monthly Revenue |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------ | ------------------- |
| All Tier 2               | 28 × $50                       | $1,400/mo           |
| 50/50 Tier 2 + Tier 3    | 14 × $50 + 14 × $150           | $2,800/mo           |
| 25/75 Tier 2 + Tier 3    | 7 × $50 + 21 × $150            | $3,500/mo           |
| Shift to Tier 4 packages | 4 × $50 + 8 × $150 + 4 × $450  | $3,200/mo           |
| **Optimal Year 3 mix**   | 5 × $50 + 12 × $150 + 6 × $450 | **$5,150/mo**       |

> **Key insight:** To grow beyond $5K/month from calls requires either adding a second practitioner or increasing Tier 4 package volume. B2B and digital products are the primary uncapped revenue levers.

## Fundraising / Investment Context

This business is designed to be **bootstrapped and profitable**. No outside investment is required or assumed.

**Break-even:** Month 2–3 (costs ~$490/month, revenue clears that easily after first B2B engagement)

**If external capital were raised** to accelerate (e.g., hire a peer support specialist):

- $25K investment would fund 1 part-time peer specialist for ~18 months
- Revenue impact: +30–40% call capacity, enabling $8,000–$10,000/month call revenue
- Payback: ~12 months on expanded revenue

## Risk Register

| Risk                                                | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation                                            |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | ------ | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Low initial traffic                                 | High       | Medium | Focus on content + 1–2 strategic partnerships         |
| B2B pipeline takes longer than 6 months             | Medium     | Medium | Don't depend on B2B in Y1; treat as upside            |
| Capacity constraint hits before higher tiers launch | Medium     | Medium | Pre-announce Tier 3 waitlist early                    |
| Platform dependency (Calendly, Stripe)              | Low        | Medium | No single-vendor lock-in; standard tools              |
| Regulatory / scope-of-practice concern              | Low        | High   | Maintain clear non-clinical framing; consult attorney |
| Founder burnout (emotionally heavy work)            | Medium     | High   | Cap calls conservatively; build in recovery time      |
