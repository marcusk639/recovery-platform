## 7. Veteran Pathway Financial Model

The veteran channel is a distinct revenue and funding stream not captured in comparable non-veteran peer support practices.

### Stream V1: VA Community Care Billing

**Prerequisites:**

1. Enroll in VA healthcare (if not already enrolled)
2. Complete VA Peer Support Specialist certification (~40–80 hours + exam; ~$750 total)
3. Apply for VA Community Care network provider status (3–6 month approval process)

**Timeline:**

- Month 6: Begin VA PSS certification process
- Month 9: Certification complete
- Month 12: VA Community Care application submitted
- Month 15 (Year 2 Q1): First VA-billed sessions

**Revenue model:**

- Medicaid peer support reimbursement: $25–$35/30-minute session (43 states cover)
- VA Community Care: ~$30/session
- Target volume: 10 sessions/month in Year 2 → 25 sessions/month in Year 3

| Year   | Monthly Sessions | Rate | Annual Revenue |
| ------ | ---------------- | ---- | -------------- |
| Year 1 | 0                | —    | $0             |
| Year 2 | 10 avg           | $30  | $3,600         |
| Year 3 | 25 avg           | $30  | $9,000         |

> **Access impact:** VA billing removes the $50/session financial barrier for veterans, expanding the addressable client pool to include low-income veterans who cannot pay out-of-pocket. This is as much mission as revenue.

### Stream V2: VSO-Sponsored Workshops and Speaking

**Sources:** VFW post community nights, American Legion chapter events, DAV local programs, military family support organizations

**Revenue model:**

- Speaking/presentation fee: $500–$1,500/event
- Workshop facilitation: $750–$2,500/session (1–3 hour format)
- Resource bundle licensing (worksheets distributed to attendees): $5/unit bulk

### Stream V3: Grant Funding (Non-Dilutive Capital)

**Sources and eligibility:**

| Funder                          | Program                                    | Est. Grant Size | Eligibility                                                                |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| SAMHSA                          | State Opioid Response (SOR) — Peer Support | $25K–$100K      | Peer support organizations; typically requires 501(c)(3) or fiscal sponsor |
| Bob Woodruff Foundation         | Veteran mental health/SUD                  | $25K–$100K      | Veteran-serving orgs; requires 501(c)(3)                                   |
| Gary Sinise Foundation          | Veteran wellness                           | $10K–$50K       | Veteran-serving nonprofits                                                 |
| VA Center for Innovation        | Veteran health innovation                  | $50K–$200K      | Novel veteran health approaches                                            |

**Strategic path to grants:**

1. **Year 1:** Establish LLC, document service delivery, build track record
2. **Year 1 Q4:** Explore fiscal sponsorship partnership with an existing 501(c)(3) to access grants without nonprofit incorporation
3. **Year 2:** Apply to 3–5 grant opportunities; realistic yield ~$25,000
4. **Year 3:** With track record, apply to larger SAMHSA and foundation grants; realistic yield ~$50,000

**Important note:** Grants are recognized as income but have zero cost of delivery. They represent the single highest-margin revenue source and should be pursued aggressively in Year 2.
